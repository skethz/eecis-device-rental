⚠ Rental rules — please read before you request

  • Initial request: To rent a device from our group, a user from another group must request approval from our lab manager (the current lab manager of Prof. Taekwang Jang's group is Seungki Hong, hongse@ethz.ch). The request must specify the rental period and give your name and the names of your own lab manager and professor.
  • Extensions: If you wish to extend the rental period, you must submit another request to our lab manager (via My rentals).
  • Returns: When returning the device, you must notify our lab manager by marking it returned in My rentals.
  • Overdue warnings: If the rental period expires and no return is recorded in the system, a warning email is sent automatically to you, your lab manager and professor, and our lab manager — every day until the return is recorded.
  • New devices: Group members who purchase a device can propose it on the Devices page; it appears in the list once our lab manager approves it.
