⚠ How proposing a device works

  1. Propose: If you have purchased a new device for the group, fill in the form below (name, maker, model, Nr. label if it has one). You get a receipt email straight away.
  2. Review: The proposal goes to our lab manager (currently Seungki Hong, hongse@ethz.ch), who receives an email with Approve/Deny links.
  3. Decision: Once approved, the device appears in the device list and can be rented; if denied, nothing changes. You are emailed the decision either way and can follow the status under Your proposals.
  4. Edits: Only the lab manager can change or deactivate existing devices. If a listed device is wrong or gone, email the lab manager.
